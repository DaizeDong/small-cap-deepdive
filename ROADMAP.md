# Roadmap

This roadmap describes current contracts and work that still needs evidence. Release
identifiers live in the package metadata. Real-run results and historical campaign records
belong in the initialized, versioned PRIVATE companion.

## Current contracts

- Configuration, reports and tracking use shared DATA/CONFIG discovery and separate PRIVATE
  worktree roots for alternate profiles. Blank SEC identity is NOT READY. Writers enforce
  source artifact ownership; [CONFIG.md](CONFIG.md) owns selection, readiness and retention.
  Synthetic checks cover declared local behavior; live capability and protected-data
  retirement require separate evidence.
- Discovery records source scope and completion. SIC tagging forwards candidates for the
  bound theme-fit stage; it does not establish theme membership. Host results and ingestion
  receipts are required to complete the workflow stages.
- Financial values retain dated evidence, compatible annual periods and units. Missing
  evidence remains unavailable. Debt and lease evidence determine whether an EV or NAV
  calculation is supportable.
- Eligibility uses explicit financial, concentration, current-loss and source-integrity
  guards. The active margin of safety must be available; the conservative FCF policy
  requires intrinsic equity at least 30% above market cap for its numeric BUY threshold.
- Diagnostic signals do not change eligibility, ratings, implied probabilities or current
  scoring. Catalyst labels remain unverified discovery hints. The catalyst MoS waiver
  remains frozen pending mechanism verification and category-specific evidence.
- Finalization and ranking preserve existing artifacts. Tracking separates precise returns,
  missing outcomes, review coverage and calibration. A fresh or unscored ledger has unknown
  calibration.

These are implementation contracts, not claims of predictive performance. See
[evidence status](docs/evidence-status.md) for execution and research acceptance limits.

## Priorities

### Financial and forensic evidence

Extend dated evidence for accruals, diluted-share growth, share-based compensation and the
net-income/cash-flow gap. Any proposed rating threshold needs a stated mechanism, synthetic
boundary cases and separately validated observations.

Evaluate proposed changes to source-mismatch or debt-quality eligibility rules without
assuming an earlier frequency or return statistic establishes the answer. Their current
policy remains in force until a reviewed change and its validation are available.

### Form 4 and catalyst verification

The default insider source is OpenInsider. EDGAR Form 4 mode is currently unavailable.
Implement and verify transaction direction across ordinary and derivative transactions,
gifts and amendments. Generated fixtures must distinguish valid, unavailable, partial and
malformed observations before live comparisons are assessed.

Catalyst verification should record its mechanism, trigger date and time remaining. A
discovery label or an insider count alone cannot release the MoS waiver.

### Population and theme coverage

Expand dated eligible gold sets and report discovery recall, final recall and loss attribution
separately. Caps, missing pages and optional SIC recall limit population claims.

Sector-specific gate rules need explicit configuration and measured admission behavior.
Financial-adjacent businesses, biotechnology and overlapping energy classifications require
careful review without turning an SIC hint into an unsupported exclusion.

### Metadata and workflow continuity

Audit financial fiscal-year labels and liquidity provenance end to end. Preserve the selected
artifact identity through theme-fit, deepdive and finalization. Avoid redundant acquisition
only when the reused result is bound to the current request and its scope remains valid.

### Calibration and future automation

Assess mature outcomes with sample composition, uncertainty and missing coverage. Neither a
fixed number of verdicts nor a populated scorecard alone establishes predictive performance.
Any change to the diagnostic role of a signal requires a separate evidence-based decision.

Automated theme selection remains deferred. Revisit it only through a defined comparison
against an appropriate baseline with dated populations and realized outcomes.
